Privacy

This page explains what SpendLens stores on your device, when data is sent to other services, and how this support website is hosted.

Updated: 22 October 2026

Publisher and contact

SpendLens is operated by Niklas Nordkamp and published from the Niki123456123456/SpendLens repository. For support and privacy questions, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub Issues. Issues are public: do not include receipts, account credentials or other personal information.

What stays on your device

The iOS app stores purchase details (retailer, branch address, date, item names, quantities, categories and EUR amounts), selected receipt photos and profile information in local app storage. Manual receipt management works without an account. SpendLens does not automatically sync your receipts to a developer-operated cloud service.

Optional ChatGPT connection and AI reading

If you connect ChatGPT, sign-in and authorization are handled by OpenAI. The native iOS module keeps authentication tokens in the iOS Keychain and uses account metadata to separate local profiles. When you choose “Read with AI”, the selected receipt photo and extraction instructions are sent directly from your iPhone to OpenAI to extract receipt details. Receipt photos can contain names, addresses, purchase history or payment information. Check and, where necessary, obscure personal information before sending. The extracted result is shown for review and saved locally only when you save the purchase.

OpenAI processing

The optional AI feature uses an existing third-party account and requires the appropriate authorization. Processing and retention by OpenAI depend on the service, applicable account terms and your settings. SpendLens does not promise that OpenAI immediately deletes submitted content or excludes it from all logging. Read OpenAI’s privacy information and the terms applicable to your account before enabling this feature. You can use manual entry without sending receipt photos to OpenAI.

Permissions, sharing and deletion

Camera and photo-library access are used to obtain receipt photos at your request. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings. A JSON export is created only when you initiate it and is shared through the iOS share sheet with the destination you select; it excludes receipt images and account credentials. Delete a purchase in the app to remove its saved record and associated local photo. Guest, demo and connected-account profiles have separate local records. Signing out removes access tokens but does not delete saved purchases or revoke the authorization held by OpenAI. Manage that authorization in ChatGPT. Device backups and previously exported files are controlled separately by you and your device settings.

Analytics and advertising

SpendLens does not include an advertising or analytics SDK and does not connect to your bank account. Local sample data is provided for trying the app without signing in.

This website and GitHub support

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not set its own tracking cookies, load analytics tools, use contact forms or load third-party fonts. GitHub can process technical connection data such as IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure the website. If you open a GitHub issue, GitHub processes the information you provide and the issue is publicly visible. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices, retention and contact options.

Questions about your data

For questions about SpendLens or requests concerning information you have sent to support, contact the repository maintainer. Because receipt records are local, the maintainer cannot retrieve or delete them remotely; use the app’s export and deletion controls. For data held by OpenAI or GitHub, contact the relevant provider using its privacy channels. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent and complain to a supervisory authority.
