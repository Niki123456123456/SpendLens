FAQ

A few useful answers.

Do I need an account?

No. Manual entry, receipt management and spending insights work without an account. Use “Try with sample data” to explore the app.

How do I change the app language?

Open iOS Settings → Apps → SpendLens → Language and choose German or English. You can also open these settings from the Profile tab.

What does AI receipt reading require?

Connect an eligible ChatGPT account in Profile and authorize plan usage. Then choose a receipt photo and tap “Read with AI”. Check all extracted details before saving. Manual entry is always available.

How do I export or delete my data?

In Profile, choose “Export all receipts as JSON” and save the file from the share sheet. To remove a purchase and its saved photo, open the receipt, choose “Delete purchase” and confirm.

Why are my receipts missing after signing out?

Guest, demo and connected-account profiles use separate local storage. Return to the corresponding profile to see its receipts. Reinstalling the app can remove local data, so export anything you want to keep.
