SpendLens 1.0.0 — App Review notes

Core features work without signing in. On first launch, choose "Try with sample data" on the Overview screen to explore sample receipts. Tabs: Overview, Receipts, Insights, Profile.

To enter a purchase without an account: Overview > Scan receipt > Enter purchase manually. Enter the retailer, date (YYYY-MM-DD), EUR receipt total and item details, then save. The receipt and monthly insights are available immediately. Receipts are stored locally; the Profile tab offers JSON export on iOS.

German and English follow the iOS per-app language setting.

SpendLens does not create or authenticate a primary app account. All real receipts use the same device-local archive, including when ChatGPT is connected, disconnected, switched or unavailable. Demo data is separate and independent of the connection.

The optional "Connect ChatGPT for AI reading" connection uses an existing eligible third-party ChatGPT account for AI receipt reading. It is not required for local receipt management. The user must explicitly authorize eligible ChatGPT plan usage. "Read with AI" opens a consent sheet for every analysis, including retries. It identifies OpenAI (ChatGPT) as the recipient, describes the entire receipt image and any visible personal, purchase or payment information, explains the purpose, and links to both privacy policies. Only "Agree and send this photo" starts transmission. "Cancel – send nothing" or dismissing the sheet sends no receipt image. The sheet also explains that manual entry is available without AI. Consent is not remembered between analyses. No reviewer credentials are supplied; please use sample data or manual entry to review the account-free features.
