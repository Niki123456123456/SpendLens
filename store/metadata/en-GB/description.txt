More clarity. Less paper.

SpendLens helps you keep track of your everyday purchases. Photograph a receipt or enter it manually. Review the retailer, branch, date and items, then save your purchase on your device.

YOUR PURCHASES IN ONE PLACE
• Take receipt photos or choose them from your photo library
• Enter purchases manually without an account
• Review quantities, unit prices, discounts and categories
• Search by retailer, item or date
• Explore monthly spending by category
• Keep original receipt photos with your purchases
• Export receipts as JSON on iOS

OPTIONAL AI RECEIPT READING
Connect your eligible ChatGPT account to read receipt photos and categorize items. A selected receipt photo is sent to OpenAI only when you choose to read it with AI. This feature requires the appropriate ChatGPT authorization. Check all extracted details before saving.

YOUR DATA ON YOUR DEVICE
Purchases and receipt photos are stored locally. SpendLens does not require a bank connection or automatically sync your purchases to the cloud.

GERMAN AND ENGLISH
Change the app language in iOS Settings. Purchases are recorded in EUR.
